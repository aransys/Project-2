    About Music Explorer

    Music Explorer is a web app built to help you discover music quickly. Search the Deezer catalog, preview tracks, save your favorites, and keep things sorted the way you like.

    • Instant previews with a sleek mini-player
    • Save favorites locally — they persist between visits
    • Keyboard shortcuts: Space play/pause, ←/→ tracks, ↑/↓ volume, M mute, F favorite, / focus search
    • Light & dark themes, respects your system preference
    • Fully responsive and built with accessibility in mind
